Problem Statement and Understanding

India's urban centers house millions of tech builders, yet grassroots event discovery remains broken and fragmented.

01

Fragmented Discovery

  • Trapped in Silos: 85%+ of tech meetups, hackathons, and cultural jams live in closed WhatsApp groups or ephemeral Instagram stories.
  • Zero Central Hub: No single platform tracks grassroots, community-driven gatherings.
  • Massive Opportunity Cost: Builders and founders miss critical networking happening 2 km away.
  • Organizer Invisibility: First-time indie organizers struggle to reach audiences outside their immediate contact list.
02

The "Hyperlocal" Void

  • City-Wide Is Broken: Legacy platforms list events for an entire metro (e.g. "Mumbai" or "Bengaluru").
  • Transit Nightmare: An event 35 km away across town is practically inaccessible during peak traffic hours.
  • The Missing Question: No platform answers: "What tech or creator events are within 5 to 10 km of me today?"
  • Lack of Real Proximity: Absence of live GPS radius filters calibrated for Indian urban commutes.
03

High Organizer Friction

  • Hostile Setup: Legacy platforms demand coordinate lookups, lengthy approval queues, or steep monthly fees ($35+/mo).
  • Costly API Traps: Commercial map APIs (Mapbox, Google Maps SDK) force unsustainable fees on free events.
  • Our Solution Understanding: We need sub-50ms GPS radius filtering paired with effortless 60-second creation via plain Google Maps links.
Mandatory Slide 02 • Architecture
02 / 05

Technical Approach

A modern, scalable architecture coupling Next.js 14 App Router with Supabase's PostGIS spatial calculation engine.

UI

Frontend & Apple HIG

  • Next.js 14 App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4.
  • Apple HIG tactile craft: glassmorphism (24px blur), spring physics micro-interactions.
  • Dual Light & Dark modes with WCAG AAA contrast compliance.
  • Browser Geolocation + Haversine distance computations.
DB

PostGIS Spatial Engine

  • Supabase PostgreSQL with native PostGIS spatial extension.
  • Stored procedure get_nearby_events using ST_DWithin & ST_Distance.
  • GiST spatial indexes on geography(Point, 4326) for sub-50ms spatial lookups.
  • Pre-seeded coordinate anchors for 5 major Indian metros.
MAP

Maps Link Parser

  • Proprietary URL extractor in lib/maps.ts.
  • Accepts any Google Maps link (place, venue pin, directions, coordinates).
  • Zero geocoding API keys required — saves 100% of mapping API costs.
  • Generates direct one-click turn-by-turn navigation URLs.
SEC

Auth & Real Checkout

  • Real Supabase Auth (Email OTP, Password, OAuth).
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies protecting RSVPs, events, and community walls.
  • Apple-styled payment checkout supporting UPI, Cards, and Netbanking.
  • Instant QR Digital Ticket generation & calendar exports (.ics / Google).
